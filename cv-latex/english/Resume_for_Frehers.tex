\begin{rSection}{Research Experience}

{\bf Data World: Scaling Data Environments from Scratch \quad \textit{First Author}} \hfill {\em May 2026 -- Present} \\
Proposed \textbf{Data World}, an engine that \textbf{synthesizes data-agent training environments and tasks from scratch} with explicit world mechanisms, and released the open-source \textbf{DataWorld-Bench} benchmark and \textbf{DataWorld-35B-A3B} model. \textbf{RL-zero} training without trajectory distillation improves \textbf{DAComp} and \textbf{JobBench} by \textbf{14.3} and \textbf{12.1} points, respectively, among other benchmarks, validating the synthesized data quality, and is verified effective on the \textbf{LongCat 2.0/2.5} model series. \\[-2pt]
{\scriptsize \textbf{Page:} \url{https://zhanli-li.github.io/Data-World/} \quad \textbf{Code:} \url{https://github.com/Zhanli-Li/Data-World} \quad \textbf{Data:} \url{https://huggingface.co/collections/Zhanli-Li/dataworld}}\\[-6pt]

{\bf DeepRead: Document Structure-Aware Reasoning to Enhance Agentic Search \quad \textit{First Author}} \hfill {\em October 2025 -- February 2026} \\
Proposed \textbf{DeepRead}, a structure-aware document reasoning agent that preserves layout fidelity via OCR, constructs \textbf{paragraph-level coordinates}, and equips LLMs with \textbf{Retrieve} and \textbf{ReadSection} tools. Unlike flat chunk-based agentic search, DeepRead explicitly models document hierarchy and reading order, allowing the agent to first locate relevant regions and then inspect complete local context. Under our environment setting, this gives rise to human-like ``locate-then-read'' reading behavior and improves over \textbf{Search-o1} style baselines by an average of \textbf{10.3\%} across four multi-type document benchmarks. \\
$\triangleright$~ \textbf{arXiv Preprint}; reported by \textbf{New Wisdom}. \\[-2pt]
{\scriptsize \textbf{Paper:} \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05014} \quad \textbf{Code:} \url{https://github.com/Zhanli-Li/DeepRead}}\\[-2pt]
{\scriptsize \textbf{Media:} \url{https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/BhvUQgREp4NOvb6axiWXiQ}}\\[-6pt]

{\bf MuDABench: Multi-Document Analytical QA Benchmark and Multi-Agent Framework \quad \textit{First Author}} \hfill {\em April 2025 -- December 2025} \\
Constructed \textbf{MuDABench}, a multi-document analytical QA benchmark with \textbf{80k+ pages}, \textbf{332 questions}, and \textbf{4,964 structured intermediate facts} that expose failure stages across document selection, extraction, cross-document aggregation, and numerical reasoning over large collections. Designed a \textbf{Multi-Agent} workflow that substantially improves over standard retrieval baselines. \\
$\triangleright$~ \textbf{Published in} \textit{ACL 2026 (CCF-A)}. \\[-2pt]
{\scriptsize \textbf{Paper:} \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22239} \quad \textbf{Code:} \url{https://github.com/Zhanli-Li/MuDABench} \quad \textbf{Data:} \url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zhanli-Li/MuDABench}}\\[-6pt]

{\bf SHAP-Based Causal Inference Framework for Corporate Finance \quad \textit{First Author}} \hfill {\em September 2024 -- January 2025} \\
Built an interpretable machine learning framework combining manually coded panel data and \textbf{Optuna} hyperparameter optimization, achieving $R^2=0.76$ on corporate total factor productivity prediction. Further used \textbf{SHAP} to quantify the \textbf{nonlinear marginal contribution} of ESG rating disagreement to outcome variables and reveal potential causal pathways linking heterogeneous ESG signals with productivity outcomes. \\
$\triangleright$~ \textbf{Published in} \textit{Finance Research Letters} (CAS Q1 Top, JCR Q1, IF: 6.9); received the Outstanding Paper Award at the Tsinghua Causal Inference Seminar. \\[-2pt]
{\scriptsize \textbf{Paper:} \url{https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2025.107127} \quad \textbf{Media:} \url{https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/5oEEnyVM_0PdWTg-7X8RJA}}\\[-6pt]
